Memorial.

Testing · Living Wall

A timber joint only holds against an opposing force. Every named and unnamed load below is part of the frame that made this desk.

Modeled on the plain ceramic tablets in Postman’s Park, London, and the stone markers beside Lithuanian roads. No photographs of living faces, no speeches, no hierarchy between what helped and what opposed.
