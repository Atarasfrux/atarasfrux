Wall of Memory.

Testing · Living Wall

Compiled from white to black and from calculating ego to No-I. Background luminance falls while syntax ink reverses polarity so opposing forces hold sharp contrast across the entire descent.

Modeled on the ceramic wall in Postman’s Park, London, and stone markers beside Lithuanian roads. No photographs of living faces, no unconsented names. Every register stays bolted to silence, sound, and smell.
